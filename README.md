A beautifully crafted, high-precision web application to help you find the Qibla direction from anywhere in the world. Designed with ❤️ for a seamless, intuitive experience.

---

### ✨ Features

- **🎯 Real-time Precision**: Uses your device's GPS and magnetometer for live heading updates.
- **📱 Mobile-First Design**: Optimized for a smooth experience on all devices, especially mobile compasses.
- **💎 Premium Aesthetics**: Featuring a sleek dark mode with emerald accents and glassmorphism.
- **🔄 Hybrid Compass**: A unique rotating dial system that moves with your phone, just like a physical compass.
- **🛰️ Geospatial Accuracy**: Calculated using the Great Circle Distance (Haversine-like) formula for exact bearings.

### 🗺️ How it Works

The app calculates the Qibla bearing from your current coordinates (Latitude/Longitude) to the Kaaba in Makkah (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E). It then overlays this bearing onto your device's real-time orientation to guide you accurately.

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** For the best accuracy on mobile, calibrate your compass by moving your phone in a figure-8 motion and ensure you're away from large metal objects!
